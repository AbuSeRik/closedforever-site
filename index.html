Closed Forever uploader

Closed Forever is a YouTube channel about abandoned places in the United States: amusement parks, malls, hotels and towns that shut down.

The Closed Forever uploader is a private tool that the channel owner uses to upload finished videos to the channel. It sets each video's title, description, thumbnail and publish time through the YouTube Data API.

The tool works only with the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.
